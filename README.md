# Wash&Fold 🧺
### Professional Laundry & Linen Care Solutions

Wash&Fold is a professional laundry and linen management business providing reliable laundry solutions for hotels, hospitality businesses, commercial establishments, and individual customers.

Our goal is to combine professional laundry operations with technology to deliver consistent quality, efficient operations, transparent pricing, and excellent customer service.

---

## 🏢 About the Business

Wash&Fold operates laundry facilities in **Lucknow and Ayodhya, Uttar Pradesh, India**, with a focus on B2B hotel laundry services and commercial linen management.

We work with hospitality businesses to manage their daily laundry requirements, ensuring clean, fresh, hygienic, and professionally finished linen.

## 🧺 Our Services

- **Hotel Laundry:** Daily washing and processing of hotel linen.
- **Commercial Laundry:** Bulk laundry solutions for businesses.

## B2C Pricing Site

The B2C laundry pricing page is a dependency-free static site in `index.html`, `styles.css`, and `services.js`.

To publish it with Cloudflare Pages, connect this repository, choose the **None** framework preset, leave the build command blank, and use `/` as the build output directory. Every push to `main` will then deploy automatically.

To add or change a service, copy an existing price card in `index.html` and update its name, description, price, and `aria-label`. The cards are intentionally static so they render without JavaScript.
